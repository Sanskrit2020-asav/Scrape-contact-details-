## Who we are
North Nepal Travel & Trek is a Kathmandu-based trekking and tour operator with
over 15 years of experience arranging treks, peak climbing, helicopter tours and
cultural journeys across Nepal. We are government-licensed and work with
experienced, insured local guides and porters.

## What we arrange
- Teahouse treks: Everest Base Camp, Annapurna Base Camp, Annapurna Circuit,
  Langtang Valley, Manaslu Circuit, Upper Mustang, Ghorepani Poon Hill.
- Peak climbing: Island Peak, Mera Peak (NMA trekking peaks).
- Helicopter tours and scenic mountain flights.
- Cultural tours, Chitwan jungle safari, Pokhara extensions.

## How we price
Quotations are built per group and include guide, porter, permits, accommodation,
meals on trek, domestic flights where needed, transfers and government taxes.
The authoritative source for prices is the company cost-estimator spreadsheet;
the assistant defers to it when loaded.

## Booking & policies (summary)
- A 20% deposit confirms the booking; the balance is due on arrival in Kathmandu.
- Travel insurance covering high-altitude trekking AND helicopter evacuation is
  mandatory.
- Permits and domestic flights are non-refundable once issued.

## Safety
We plan conservative acclimatisation schedules, carry a comprehensive first-aid
kit, monitor for altitude sickness daily, and maintain helicopter-evacuation
contacts for emergencies.
